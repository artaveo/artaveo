# Hi, I'm Zakir

I'm a software engineer in Kabul and the person behind **Artaveo**, a small digital development studio. I'm also a senior Computer Science student at Kateb University, with a GPA above 90.

Most of my work is software for real businesses: a ticket booking platform for a transport company, a portal for a study hall, a desktop app for a dental clinic. These are the projects I enjoy most. You sit with the people who will use the thing, learn how their day actually goes, and build something that saves them an hour of paperwork instead of adding one.

## What I can build for you

- Professional web, desktop and mobile applications
- Management and ERP-style systems: members, billing, finance, inventory, reports, staff roles
- Automation of the manual work around an existing system (imports, reports, device integrations, backups)
- Any other software project, whether that means taking over and improving something you already have or starting from a blank page

I work in Dari and English, and I care about getting right-to-left interfaces right, not just flipped.
